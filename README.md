Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
Excel-Based Sales Dashboard Project


Overview
This project presents an Excel analysis of sales data from a coffee shop. The aim is to explore trends in product sales, store performance, and customer purchasing behavior across different periods. The analysis is conducted using Excel PivotTables, charts, and formula-based calculations.


Data Source
File Used: Copy of Coffee Shop Sales.xlsx


Format: Microsoft Excel Workbook (.xlsx)


Content: Transaction-level sales data including date, time, location, product category, product type, unit price, and quantity.


Key Steps
Data Cleaning
Checked for missing or inconsistent values.
Standardized date and time fields for analysis.
Sales Aggregation
Summarized revenue, product sales, and transactions by month, hour, and location.
Used Excel PivotTables to group and analyze data.


Trend Analysis
Identified revenue growth over months.
Tracked sales volume by product and time.


Visualization
Created line charts for monthly trends.
Used bar charts to show top products and store performance.


Key Findings
Revenue Growth: Revenue consistently increased from January to June, with June being the highest-earning month.
Store Performance: Hell's Kitchen was the top-performing location, followed closely by Astoria and Lower Manhattan.
Best-Selling Products: Coffee and Tea were the most frequently sold categories. Barista Espresso led in total revenue.
Sales Timing: Most transactions occurred between 7 AM and 11 AM, with a peak at 10 AM.

# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis  
### Excel-Based Sales Dashboard Project

---

## 📊 Overview  
This project presents an Excel analysis of sales data from a coffee shop. The aim is to explore trends in product sales, store performance, and customer purchasing behavior across different periods. The analysis is conducted using **Excel PivotTables**, **charts**, and **formula-based calculations**.

---

## 📁 Data Source  
- **File Used**: `Copy of Coffee Shop Sales.xlsx`  
- **Format**: Microsoft Excel Workbook (`.xlsx`)  
- **Content**: Transaction-level sales data including:
  - Date & time  
  - Store location  
  - Product category & type  
  - Unit price  
  - Quantity sold

---

## 🛠️ Key Steps  

### 1. Data Cleaning  
- Checked for missing or inconsistent values  
- Standardized date and time fields for analysis  

### 2. Sales Aggregation  
- Summarized revenue, product sales, and transactions  
- Used **Excel PivotTables** for monthly, hourly, and location-based analysis  

### 3. Trend Analysis  
- Identified monthly revenue growth  
- Tracked sales volume by product and time  

### 4. Visualization  
- Line charts for monthly sales trends  
- Bar charts for product and store performance  

---

## 📌 Key Findings  

- **Revenue Growth**: Consistent increase from January to June.  
- **Top Store**: *Hell's Kitchen* led in revenue, followed by *Astoria* and *Lower Manhattan*.  
- **Best-Selling Products**:  
  - **By Quantity**: Coffee and Tea  
  - **By Revenue**: Barista Espresso  
- **Sales Timing**: Most transactions occurred between **7 AM and 11 AM**, peaking at **10 AM**.
